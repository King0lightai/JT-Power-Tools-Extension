Auto Collapse completed.

Schedule groups that are 100% done collapse on page load. You see what still needs your attention — not what's already finished.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Open a schedule and any group that's at 100% complete is already collapsed. Active groups stay open. You spend zero time scrolling past finished phases looking for what's next.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Auto Collapse Completed Groups under Schedule & Calendar.
  3. Flip it on.

How to use it

  1. Open any job's Schedule, Tasks, or To-Dos page.
  2. Done groups are already collapsed. Active groups are expanded.
  3. Need to look at a finished group? Just click it to expand. It'll stay open until you navigate away.

Good to know

  • Works on both Gantt and List views.
  • Re-applies when you switch jobs or hit back/forward.
  • Pairs nicely with Freeze Header so your toolbar stays put while you scroll.
