Custom theme.

Pick your own colors for JobTread's interface — primary, background, and text. Save up to three full themes and swap between them whenever you want.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Three colors to choose: primary (the accent color), background (the main canvas), and text. Pick them in a color picker, click Apply, and JobTread takes on the look. Save themes by name — "Office", "Field", "Night Shift" — and switch with one click.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Custom Theme under Appearance.
  3. Flip it on. The Theme tab in the popup unlocks.

How to make a theme

  1. Open the Theme tab in the popup.
  2. Click each color box and pick your color (or pick a Quick Theme as a starting point).
  3. Click Apply Theme to see it on JobTread live.
  4. Like it? Click a Save slot, give it a name, save.
  5. Switch themes anytime by clicking Load on a saved slot.

Good to know

  • Make sure your text color contrasts with your background or things become hard to read.
  • Yellow edit highlights and task status colors stay visible on top of any custom theme.
  • Turning on Custom Theme automatically turns off Dark Mode and Contrast Fix — only one appearance mode at a time.
