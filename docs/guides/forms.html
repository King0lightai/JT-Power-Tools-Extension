Forms.

Fill out your team's checklists, inspections, and sign-offs right on the job — in a drawer, with auto-save and PDF export.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Forms adds a Forms button to the job action bar — right alongside Edit Job, Message, Document, and Task. Click it and a drawer slides out with your org's form templates for that job: punch lists, safety checklists, inspections, change-order intake, client sign-offs — whatever your team uses.

Fill it in and your answers save themselves as you go. When the form's done, export it to PDF for the file or to send along. No more separate apps or paper that never makes it back to the office.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Forms under Documents & Text and flip it on (Power User).
  3. Make sure you're signed in on the Account tab — Forms needs your account to load your org's templates.

How to use it

  1. Open any job and click Forms in the action bar. On a phone or narrow window, it lives in the “⋯” overflow menu with the other actions.
  2. Pick a form. If the job has only one available, it opens straight to it; if there are several, you choose from a list.
  3. Fill in the fields. Your answers auto-save — you'll see a “Saved … ago” note as you work.
  4. When you're done, export the completed form to PDF.

Good to know

  • The Forms button only shows on job pages, and it's styled to match JobTread's own buttons so it feels native.
  • Auto-save handles teamwork: if someone else updated the same form while you had it open, your changes merge and a note tells you which fields refreshed.
  • Switch to a different job (or leave the job) with the drawer open and it closes itself — no stray form from the last job.
  • Export to PDF whenever you need a clean copy for records, the client, or the field.
  • Power User tier, and it requires being signed in — that's how it knows your org and its templates.
