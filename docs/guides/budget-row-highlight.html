Budget Row Highlight.

Drop a colored circle into a budget row and the whole row lights up in that color. A real highlighter for a budget that never had one.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

JobTread budgets have no way to highlight a row — so people improvise. Budget Row Highlight turns that improvisation into a real feature: type one of nine colored circle emojis anywhere in a row and the entire row — every cell, including the sticky number and name columns — gets a soft color band.

Use it to flag what needs attention, group line items by trade or phase, mark approvals, or triage a long budget at a glance. The tints are tuned to stay easy on the eyes and keep your text readable, and they automatically adapt to light, Dark Mode, and Custom Theme.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Budget Row Highlight under Budget.
  3. Flip the toggle on. No reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Open any job's Budget.
  2. Type a colored circle emoji into a row — the description, the name, or a custom-field cell all work.
  3. The whole row tints in that color. Change the color by swapping the emoji; clear it by deleting the emoji.

The nine colors, in priority order, are 🔴 red, 🟠 orange, 🟡 amber, 🟢 green, 🔵 blue, 🟣 purple, 🟤 brown, ⚫ black, and ⚪ white. If a row happens to hold more than one circle, the first color in that order wins.

Good to know

  • Selecting a row steps the tint aside so JobTread's blue selection highlight shows through — your color comes right back when you deselect.
  • The row you're actively editing drops its tint while the cursor is in it, so the caret never washes out against the color. It re-tints the moment you click away.
  • It layers on top of Budget Hierarchy Shading — flagged rows show your color, everything else keeps its group shading.
  • Tints adapt automatically to light, Dark Mode, and Custom Theme, so colors stay legible whichever look you run.
