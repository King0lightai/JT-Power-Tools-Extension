What it does

JT Power Tools is free to install. Ten features are free forever. The paid tiers add productivity (Essential), customization (Pro), and API-powered tools plus AI integration (Power User). One subscription seat covers your whole organization — your team members create their own accounts under your license.

Pick a tier

  1. Free — 10 tools including Dark Mode, Text Formatter, Auto Sum, Contrast Fix, Kanban Filter, Auto Collapse, Fat Gantt, and more. No account needed.
  2. Essential ($10/mo) — adds Quick Notes, Smart Resize, Freeze Header, PDF Markup Tools.
  3. Pro ($20/mo) — adds Schedule Task Checkboxes, Custom Theme, Preview Mode, Availability Filter, Reverse Thread Order, plus the Tweaks Engine.
  4. Power User ($30/mo) — adds Job Switcher Filter, Budget Changelog, Unassigned Availability, multi-org support, and the MCP server (AI integration).

Buy and activate

  1. Visit jtpowertools.com/#pricing and pick your tier.
  2. Check out via Gumroad. You'll get a license key by email.
  3. Register at app.jtpowertools.com with the license key.
  4. Invite your team from the portal — they create their own logins under your license.
  5. Each person installs the extension and signs in with their email and password. Premium features unlock automatically.

Good to know

  • Try free features first — you might be surprised how much you get without paying.
  • Annual billing on each tier saves money vs. monthly.
  • 7-day free trial on every paid tier. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee.
