Power User

Unassigned availability.

Adds a TASKS row to the Availability view showing every task with no assignee yet. Filter by Labor, Material, Equipment. Click a task to assign it on the spot.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

In the Availability view you can see what your team is doing. But you can't see what's not assigned to anyone — and that's where scheduling gaps hide. This adds a TASKS row at the top of the grid showing every task that's missing an assignee. A filter bar at the top lets you toggle task types (Labor, Material, Equipment, etc.) on and off. Click a task card and its sidebar opens — assign it, done.

Turn it on

  1. You need a JobTread Grant Key — set this up under Account on the popup first.
  2. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  3. Find Unassigned Availability under Schedule & Calendar.
  4. Flip it on. Go to Schedule and switch to Availability.

How to use it

  1. TASKS row appears at the top of the availability grid.
  2. Above it, the filter bar shows task type chips. Click All or None to start, then click chips to fine-tune.
  3. Spot a gap or an unassigned task you can place? Click the card.
  4. The task's sidebar opens — assign it to someone right there.
  5. Hit Refresh if new tasks were just created and you don't see them yet.

Good to know

  • Hit All first, then uncheck types that aren't relevant for who you're scheduling.
  • Collapse the filter bar after you've set it up to save screen space.
  • Run this every Monday morning to find the week's unassigned work fast.
