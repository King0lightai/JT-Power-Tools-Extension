Invoice Forecast.

Turn JobTread's invoice schedule into a month-by-month cash-flow picture — paid, committed, and projected across every active job, right inside Reports.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

JobTread links invoices to schedule tasks, but it never charts them — the only way to see when money is set to come in is to open each task and spot a dollar amount. Invoice Forecast adds an Invoice Forecast tab to JobTread's Reports that does it for you: a month-by-month bar chart of upcoming invoices across every active job, dated by each invoice's scheduled task.

Each month's bar stacks three layers, so it's a forecast and a schedule at once:

  • Paid — invoices you've already collected on, including deposits you bill on approval that have no scheduled task. Dated by their issue date.
  • Committed — unpaid invoices on jobs that are sold (an approved contract).
  • Projected — unpaid invoices on jobs you haven't closed yet.

Hover any bar for the per-job breakdown, and read every invoice — its day, job, amount, document status, and type — in the detail table below the chart.

Turn it on

  1. Invoice Forecast is a Power User feature — make sure your company is on that tier and you're signed in.
  2. In JobTread, open Reports. You'll see a new Invoice Forecast tab alongside the built-in reports.
  3. The first time you open it, a short setup step asks you to tell it how your company bills (below).

Set it up for your company

Power Tools serves every company, so nothing is hardcoded. Two quick choices — remembered per company — teach the forecast how you work:

  1. Which task type(s) mark an invoice. Pick the schedule task type(s) you use to represent an invoice release (for example, an "Invoice" or "Draw" task type). The chart dates each invoice by that task's start date.
  2. Which contract means a job is "sold." Pick the approved contract document name(s) that signal a sold job (for example, "Home Improvement Agreement"). Jobs with that approved document count as committed; everything else is projected.

If you run more than one company through Power Tools, each gets its own setup and its own forecast — switching orgs in JobTread reloads the view for that company.

How to read it

  1. Scan the bars to see which months are heavy and which are light. Solid green is money already collected, solid teal is committed, hatched amber is projected.
  2. Check the Collected tile for what's actually landed, and use the detail table to drill into any single month — every invoice with its day, job, amount, and status.
  3. Use the From / To period filter to frame a quarter or a fiscal year. It defaults to the current month onward; clear it to see everything, including earlier deposits.
  4. Flip Include closed jobs on when jobs that closed earlier in the year still carry invoices you want in the totals.
  5. Hit Export CSV to pull the current view — month, date, job, invoice number, amount, status, and type — into a spreadsheet.

Good to know

  • The forecast reflects when cash is scheduled to land, not document approval state — so a drafted-but-scheduled invoice still appears. The Type and status columns let you tell them apart.
  • Paid invoices are counted regardless of task type — even a deposit with no scheduled task — and they're never double-counted against the scheduled layers.
  • It runs on open jobs by default. Turn on Include closed jobs only when you need closed-job invoices in a period total.
  • Empty chart? You probably haven't picked a task type yet — open the setup and choose which task type marks an invoice.
  • The whole panel follows your appearance — it themes through Custom Theme and has a neutral-grey Dark Mode; the paid / committed / projected colors stay fixed so the data reads consistently.
