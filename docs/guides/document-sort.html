Document Sort.

Click any column header on a Documents tab to sort — and it sorts the whole list, not just the rows you can see.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

JobTread's Documents tabs — on a job, a customer, or a vendor — list your estimates, invoices, bills, and purchase orders, but the columns don't sort. Document Sort makes every column header clickable: sort by name, the job or account, subject, status, or amount, ascending or descending. Click a header once to sort, click it again to flip the direction.

The catch most sorters miss: JobTread only loads a screen's worth of rows at a time. Document Sort force-loads every row on the first click, so you're sorting the complete list — not just whatever happened to be on screen.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Document Sort under Documents & Text.
  3. Flip the toggle on. No reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Open any Documents tab — a job's Documents, or the org-wide Customers or Vendors document lists.
  2. Click a column header to sort by it. A little arrow shows the direction.
  3. Click the same header again to reverse ascending / descending.

Good to know

  • Works on all three document views — Job, Customer, and Vendor tabs.
  • That first click pulls in every row before sorting, so the order is complete and trustworthy — never just the visible page.
  • Sort by whatever you're chasing: name, job/account, subject, status, or dollar amount.
