Vendor Bills.

Forward an invoice to one address and it comes back matched to a job and a cost code, waiting for someone to say yes.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Vendor invoices arrive as email attachments and turn into typing. Somebody opens the PDF, finds the job, picks the cost code, keys the amounts, and files the original somewhere it can be found again at tax time.

Your company gets its own private bills address. Forward your AP inbox to it, or have subcontractors send there directly, and each message lands in a Pending Bills queue with its attachments intact. Your AI reads the PDF, matches it to the job and cost code, and proposes the bill — your team confirms it in JobTread.

Set it up

  1. Open the portal and go to Bills Settings.
  2. Set a default cost code. Without one, bills still post but their line items arrive empty.
  3. Add the senders you trust to the approved list — your own AP address, your bookkeeper, the subcontractors who invoice you.
  4. Forward that AP mailbox to the bills address shown on the card.

How to use it

  1. A subcontractor emails an invoice. It lands in the queue with its PDF.
  2. Ask your AI to work the queue — it reads each bill, matches the vendor, job, and cost code, and reports what it found.
  3. Approve the ones that look right. The bill posts to JobTread with its line items and the original attached.

Good to know

  • Only senders you approve are accepted. The allowlist is per company, so a stray invoice from an address you have never dealt with does not create a bill.
  • The original is kept, not just the numbers. Every message and attachment is stored intact, so the source document is still there when a bill is questioned months later.
  • Nothing posts on its own. The AI proposes; a person approves. A bill you never confirm never reaches your books.
  • The defaults are per company and set once. They are what the AI applies when it maps an invoice onto cost codes.
