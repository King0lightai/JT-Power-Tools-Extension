Message counter & templates.

See message length live as you type. Save the messages you send all the time and paste them in one click instead of retyping.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Two tools, one feature. The character counter sits in JobTread message fields and turns orange then red as you near the limit. Templates let you save common messages — your email signature, project update boilerplate, late-payment follow-up — and drop them in with a single click. No more digging through old messages to copy your own text.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Message Counter & Templates under Productivity Tools.
  3. Flip it on. Click into any message field on JobTread.

How to use templates

  1. Click the templates icon in the toolbar that appears.
  2. Hit Add New Template, give it a name (e.g. "Signature"), and paste your text.
  3. Save. To use later: open templates, click the name. It pastes at your cursor.

Good to know

  • Build a template for your email signature first — you'll use it dozens of times a day.
  • Templates sync across devices once you sign in.
  • Watch the orange warning to know when to start wrapping up a long message.
