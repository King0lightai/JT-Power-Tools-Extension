Text formatter.

A Word-style toolbar inside JobTread. Bold, italic, headings, lists, tables, colors, links — all in budget descriptions, notes, and tasks.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Click into a text field anywhere in JobTread and a formatting toolbar pops up. You get bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, three heading sizes, bullets and numbered lists, links, tables, blockquotes, and color-coded text. Everything saves with the field — your team sees the formatting too.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Text Formatter under Productivity Tools.
  3. Flip it on, then click into any text field on a budget, daily log, or task.

How to use it

  1. Select some text or just put your cursor where you want to start.
  2. Click a toolbar button — Bold, Italic, Heading, list, etc. Active formatting lights up so you can see what's on.
  3. Or use keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl/Cmd + B bold, Ctrl/Cmd + I italic, Ctrl/Cmd + U underline.
  4. Click outside the field to save. Your formatting goes with it.

Good to know

  • Color-code budget descriptions: red for urgent, green for approved, etc. Easy visual scan.
  • Use headings inside long change-order descriptions to break them into sections clients can actually read.
  • Pair with Preview Mode (Pro) to see your formatting render live as you type.
