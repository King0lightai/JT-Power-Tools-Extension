Inspect for AI.

Alt-click anything in JobTread. Paste into Claude or ChatGPT. Get back a tweak that actually targets the thing you clicked.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Tweaks let you rewrite JobTread's interface — rename a label, hide a column, restyle a button. The hard part was never the tweak. It was telling an AI which element you meant, precisely enough that what it writes works on the real page.

Inspect for AI does that part for you. Alt-click an element and it copies a description to your clipboard: a stable selector for the thing you clicked, the containers around it, what sits inside it, and which page and org you were on. Paste that into any AI chat and ask for the change you want.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Open the Tweaks tab and switch Inspect for AI on.
  3. Head to app.jobtread.com — no reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Find the element you want changed and Alt-click it.
  2. Paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor — whatever you use — and say what you want: "hide this column", "rename this to Subcontractor", "make this red when it’s overdue".
  3. Take the tweak it writes back and add it in the Tweaks tab.

Good to know

  • The selectors are built to survive. JobTread's styling classes change with every release, so they're deliberately ignored in favour of structural selectors. You get something that still matches next month, not a chain of utility classes that breaks on the next deploy.
  • The capture includes the surrounding context on purpose — two panels on a page often look identical from the element alone, and the containers are what tell them apart.
  • It copies to your clipboard and stops there. Nothing is uploaded, and you choose what to paste and where.
  • Pair it with the visual tweak builder when you'd rather point and click than describe.
