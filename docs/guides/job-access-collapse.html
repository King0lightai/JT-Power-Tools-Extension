Job Access Collapse.

Collapse the parts of the Job Access panel you never read. They stay collapsed — next job, next day, next login.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

The Job Access panel on a job dashboard lists everyone who can see the job, section by section. Useful when you're setting permissions. In the way every other day of the job, pushing what you actually came for further down the page.

Job Access Collapse puts a collapse toggle on each section header. Fold up the ones you don't need and they stay folded — the state is saved, so you set it once instead of every time a page loads.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Job Access Collapse under General.
  3. Flip the toggle on. No reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Open any job dashboard and find the Job Access panel.
  2. Click the chevron on a section header to fold that section up.
  3. Click it again when you need the section back.

Good to know

  • What you collapse is remembered per section and synced with your browser profile, so it follows you to your other machines.
  • Nothing about access changes — this is purely how much of the panel you have to look at. Permissions are untouched.
  • Turning the feature off restores every section to expanded.
