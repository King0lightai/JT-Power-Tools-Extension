Essential

Freeze header.

Job name, tabs, and toolbars stay pinned at the top while you scroll. Never lose your place or scroll back up to reach a button.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

On long budget or schedule pages, the job's name and the row of tabs (Budget, Schedule, Messages, etc.) normally scroll off the top. With Freeze Header on, they stick. You can scroll all the way to the bottom of a 200-line budget and still see what job you're on and click any tab from where you are.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Freeze Header under Productivity Tools.
  3. Flip it on. Scroll any long page on a job.

Good to know

  • Works on every major job page — Budget, Schedule, Messages, Documents, Daily Logs.
  • Frozen headers get a subtle shadow so you can tell they're floating.
  • Pairs nicely with Budget Hierarchy Shading on long budgets.
