Schedule task checkboxes.

Checkboxes appear on every task card in the schedule month view. Click one and it marks the task complete in JobTread. No opening the task, no extra steps.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Normally to mark a task complete from the schedule, you have to click into it, find the status, change it, and back out. With this on, every task card in the month view (and Action Items) has a small checkbox in the corner. One click to complete, one click to reopen. Status syncs to JobTread instantly.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Schedule Task Checkboxes under Schedule & Calendar.
  3. Flip it on. Go to the Schedule month view.

How to use it

  1. Find a task card.
  2. Click the checkbox in the corner — it shows a checkmark and the task is marked complete.
  3. Click again to reopen.

Good to know

  • End of day, run through the calendar and check off everything your crew finished. Way faster than opening each task.
  • Use it during morning huddles to update status as your team reports in.
