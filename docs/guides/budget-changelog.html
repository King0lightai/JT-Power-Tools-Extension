Power User

Budget changelog.

Pick two budget backups, click compare, and get a plain-English report of what was added, deleted, and changed — with the cost impact called out.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Every change order or budget revision should leave a trail. JobTread keeps backups but doesn't tell you what's different between them. Budget Changelog reads two backups and writes you a report: green for added line items, red for deleted, yellow for changed. Totals at the top show the dollar impact. Open it in a new tab and share the link with your client or PM.

Turn it on

  1. You need a JobTread Grant Key — set this up under Account on the popup first.
  2. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  3. Find Budget Changelog under Productivity Tools.
  4. Flip it on.

How to use it

  1. Open a job's Budget.
  2. Open the Budget Backups sidebar.
  3. Check two backups you want to compare.
  4. Click Compare Selected. The report opens in a new tab.
  5. Read the additions, deletions, and modifications. The cost impact is right at the top.

Good to know

  • Take a backup before any major change order so you can document exactly what happened.
  • Use the report to verify changes match what was approved by the client.
  • Great for end-of-project reconciliation — a paper trail of every revision.
