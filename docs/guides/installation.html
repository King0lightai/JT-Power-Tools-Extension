📦 Installation & Setup

Get started with JT Power Tools in minutes

Quick Start

Installing JT Power Tools is simple. Follow the steps below to install the extension, enable free features instantly, and optionally set up a premium account for your team.

Step 1: Install from Chrome Web Store

Install from Chrome Web Store

  1. Click the link above
    Visit the JT Power Tools page on the Chrome Web Store
  2. Click "Add to Chrome"
    Click the blue "Add to Chrome" button
  3. Confirm the installation
    Accept the permissions when prompted
  4. Pin the extension (recommended)
    Click the puzzle piece icon in your toolbar and pin JT Power Tools for easy access

Step 2: Enable Free Features

JT Power Tools includes 10 free features you can start using immediately — no account or license needed.

  1. Navigate to JobTread
    Open app.jobtread.com and log into your account
  2. Open Extension Popup
    Click the JT Power Tools icon in your Chrome toolbar
  3. Toggle on features
    You'll see toggle switches organized by category — Schedule, Budget & Editing, and Appearance. Turn on the features you want.
  4. Features activate instantly
    Most features work immediately without a page reload
Recommended Free Features to Start With

Try Dark Mode for reduced eye strain, Text Formatter for rich text editing, and Budget Hierarchy Shading for easier budget navigation. All free, all instant.

Step 3: Set Up Premium (Optional)

Premium features unlock additional productivity tools, customization, and AI-powered capabilities across three tiers: Essential ($10/mo), Pro ($20/mo), and Power User ($30/mo). All tiers are per-company, not per-user.

Purchase a Subscription

  1. Visit the pricing page
    Go to jtpowertools.com/#pricing and choose a tier
  2. Complete checkout on Gumroad
    You'll receive a confirmation email with your license key

Register Your Account

  1. Go to the JT Power Tools Portal
    Visit app.jtpowertools.com/register
  2. Enter your license key
    Paste the license key from your Gumroad confirmation email
  3. Create your account
    Set up your email, display name, and password
  4. Invite team members (optional)
    From the portal dashboard, invite employees to join your organization. They'll create their own accounts under your license.

Sign Into the Extension

  1. Open the extension popup
    Click the JT Power Tools icon in your toolbar
  2. Go to the Account section
    Scroll down to the Account panel and click "Sign In"
  3. Enter your credentials
    Use the email and password you created on the portal
  4. Premium features unlock
    Features for your tier will become available immediately
Company-Wide Licensing

One subscription covers your entire company. The account owner registers on the portal, then invites team members. Each team member signs in with their own credentials — no need to share a single license key.

Permissions Explained

JT Power Tools requests minimal permissions:

Permission Purpose Required?
storage Save your feature preferences, settings, and account session locally Yes
activeTab Apply features to the current JobTread tab only when active Yes
app.jobtread.com Enable features on the JobTread application Yes
Privacy First

JT Power Tools does NOT collect, store, or transmit your JobTread data. All settings are stored locally in your browser. Premium account authentication uses secure encrypted connections. Read our Privacy Policy

Updating the Extension

When installed from the Chrome Web Store, updates are automatic. You'll receive the latest version within 24-48 hours of release.

Troubleshooting

Features Don't Work on JobTread

  • Verify you're on app.jobtread.com (not other JobTread domains)
  • Check that features are toggled ON in the popup
  • Refresh the JobTread page
  • Try removing and re-installing the extension
  • Restart your browser

Premium Features Locked After Sign-In

  • Verify your account shows the correct tier in the popup
  • Close and reopen the popup
  • Try signing out and back in
  • Refresh the JobTread page
  • Check that your Gumroad subscription is active

Can't Sign In or Reset Password

  • Make sure you're using the email you registered with on the portal
  • Use the "Forgot password?" link in the sign-in form
  • Check your spam/junk folder for the reset email
  • If you haven't registered yet, click "Create one" to set up your account

Uninstalling

How to Uninstall

  1. Open Extensions Page
    Navigate to chrome://extensions
  2. Find JT Power Tools
    Locate the extension in your list
  3. Click "Remove"
    Click the "Remove" button and confirm

What Gets Removed

  • All extension files and code
  • Your saved settings and feature preferences
  • Your local sign-in session
  • Saved custom themes

What Stays

  • Your JobTread data (completely unaffected)
  • Any formatting you applied to text (stored in JobTread, not the extension)
  • Your JT Power Tools account on the portal (just sign back in if you reinstall)
  • Your Gumroad subscription (manage at gumroad.com/library)
