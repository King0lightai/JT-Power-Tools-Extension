Tweaks.

Make JobTread work the way your team works — rename labels, hide clutter, restyle, reorder, or warn before a risky click. No code required.

What it does

Tweaks are small, personal changes you make to JobTread itself. Rename a confusing label, hide a button you never use, recolor a section, reorder a list, or pop a confirmation before someone clicks something risky. Each tweak is scoped to your org and the page you built it on, stored on our server, and synced — so what you build shows up for your team on the pages it belongs to.

The User Tweaks Engine is the on/off switch that applies your installed tweaks. There are three ways to author them, from zero-code to fully hands-on.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon and open the Tweaks tab.
  2. Turn on the User Tweaks Engine (Pro). This applies your installed tweaks on this device.
  3. Your current org shows at the top of the tab — tweaks you build are saved to that org.

Three ways to build a tweak

  1. Point-and-click — “Build a tweak.” Click the button, pick any element on a JobTread page, then choose what to do: rename text, hide it, restyle it, warn before click, sort a list, or move it. You get a live preview before you save. No JSON, no guessing.
  2. With AI — “Copy element for AI.” Pick an element and it copies a ready-to-paste prompt describing it. Paste that into your own AI chat, get a tweak back, then hit “Paste from AI” to install it. “Copy across views for AI” grabs the same kind of element across several views (table, Gantt, month) into one combined prompt.
  3. By hand — “Blank.” For advanced users who want to write the tweak JSON themselves.

Good to know

  • Tweaks are scoped to one org and the page you made them on — they won't leak onto unrelated screens.
  • They're cached on your device too, so they keep applying even offline or before you've signed in.
  • Safety guard: a tweak can't relabel a destructive or financial button — Approve, Delete, Pay, Send, Sign, and the like — into something misleading.
  • Tweaks can shade rows by due date — for example, tint overdue Action Items red and the next few days amber — by matching on each row's date. Build it with AI (“Copy element for AI”) or by hand.
  • The engine toggle is per-device. Flip it off to instantly suspend every tweak on this machine without deleting anything.
  • Power User tier adds MCP-powered authoring — your AI can write and edit tweaks on the fly through the JobTread connection.
  • Need help from support? “Copy for support” drops a diagnostic summary on your clipboard.
