Budget hierarchy.

Nested budget groups get progressively darker shading. One look and you see exactly which line items belong to which group, even on big multi-phase projects.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

Top-level budget groups stay light. Each level deeper gets a slightly darker shade — five levels in total. Line items inherit the shade of their parent group. The whole budget reads like a clean outline instead of a flat list.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon.
  2. Find Budget Hierarchy Shading under Appearance.
  3. Flip it on. Open any job budget.

Good to know

  • Especially helpful for projects with multiple phases or trades stacked into one budget.
  • Pair with Freeze Header so your toolbar stays put while you scroll deep budgets.
  • Shading inverts under Dark Mode so contrast stays sharp.
