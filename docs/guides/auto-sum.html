Auto Sum.

Tick a few budget rows. See live totals — cost, price, and profit — without doing any math.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

When you select line items in a budget, a "Selection Totals" panel pops up at the top of the Mass Budget Actions sidebar. It shows extended cost, extended price, and profit (with margin %). The numbers update live as you check more rows on or off — perfect for sanity-checking phase totals or pricing a partial scope.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Auto Sum under Productivity Tools.
  3. Flip the toggle on. No reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Open any job's Budget page.
  2. Click the row numbers on the left to select line items. The Mass Budget Actions sidebar opens.
  3. Look at the top of that sidebar — your Selection Totals panel is right there.
  4. Add or remove rows. The totals update instantly.

Good to know

  • Profit turns green when positive, red when negative — easy gut check while you scope.
  • Items marked TBD are skipped, and you'll see a count so nothing's hidden from you.
  • Group header rows aren't double-counted — only real line items get summed.
