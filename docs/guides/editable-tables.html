Editable Tables.

Fill in a whole column of custom fields from the grid — without opening a single job.

Video walkthrough coming soon

What it does

You build a job view with the custom fields you care about — Project Manager, Designer, PDA Average — and then you can only read them. Updating twelve jobs means twelve jobs opened, twelve fields found, twelve tabs closed.

Editable Tables makes those custom field columns editable where they already are. Click the pencil on a cell or Alt+click the cell, type, and press Enter — the value goes straight to JobTread. Press Tab instead and it saves and moves to the next editable cell, so you can walk down a column and fill the whole thing in one pass.

Turn it on

  1. Click the JT Power Tools icon in your browser toolbar.
  2. Find Editable Tables under General.
  3. Flip the toggle on. No reload needed.

How to use it

  1. Open a job view in the Data Browser — /jobs or any saved view built on it.
  2. Hover a custom field cell. A pencil appears; click it, or Alt+click the cell itself.
  3. Type the new value. Enter saves it. Tab saves it and jumps to the next editable cell down the column. Esc backs out without saving.

Good to know

  • Why Alt+click and not double-click: in the Data Browser the whole row is a link, so an ordinary click opens the job. Alt+click and the pencil are the two ways to edit a cell without navigating away.
  • Only custom fields, and only ones it can verify. A column becomes editable when the view proves it maps to a real custom field. Native columns — name, address, dates, totals — are left alone, and any column it can't line up with confidence is skipped rather than risked.
  • Job views today. Task and cost-item grids aren't in scope yet.
  • Writes go through JobTread's API, so your org needs a grant key configured.
