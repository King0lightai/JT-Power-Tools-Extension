## Product description (main body)

You run your business in JobTread. JT Power Tools makes every hour you spend
there faster, clearer, and — now — a lot smarter.

It's a browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) that upgrades app.jobtread.com
in place: better formatting, dark mode, frozen table headers, PDF markup, budget
tools, custom themes, keyboard navigation, and 30+ more improvements your team
feels on day one. No exports, no new tabs, no "other system." Your data never
leaves JobTread.

**Pick the tier that fits how you work** — every tier includes everything
below it, and one subscription covers your whole company:

### Free — try it forever, $0
Install the extension and get the everyday essentials at no cost: text
Formatter, Dark Mode, Contrast Fix, Budget Hierarchy, Gantt Lines,
Auto-Collapse, Kanban Filter, Character Counter, Document Sort, Budget Auto-Sum,
and Job Access Collapse. No license needed.

### Essential — $10/mo
Everything Free, plus the daily drivers: **Quick Notes** (a synced notepad that
follows you through JobTread), **Smart Resize**, **Freeze Header** for long
tables, **PDF Markup**, **Budget Row Highlight**, and your **Org Logo** across
the app.

### Pro — $20/mo
Everything Essential, plus the power features: **Schedule & Task Checkboxes**,
**Custom Themes**, **Preview Mode** (live markdown), **Reverse Thread Order**,
**Availability Filter**, and the **Tweak Engine + Builder** — reshape JobTread's
UI your way, no code required — with **Inspect for AI** to help you build tweaks.

### Power User — $30/mo
Everything Pro, plus the API-powered layer: **MCP / AI access** (connect Claude,
ChatGPT, or Cursor to your JobTread data through our MCP server), **Custom Field
Filter**, **Budget Changelog**, **Task Type Filter**, **Per-Job Forms**, **Pave
Capture**, **Invoice Forecast**, and **AI Knowledge**.

### 🤖 Assistant — $99/mo, covers your whole company

Your team has seen AI that can talk. This is AI that **knows your jobs.**

The JT Power Tools Assistant lives in a side panel right inside JobTread. Open
it on any job and ask — it already knows what's on your screen, and it answers
from your live data, not from memory:

- **"Check this job."** One click and it audits the job you're looking at:
  change orders that were approved but never invoiced, budget lines running
  over estimate, tasks slipping past baseline, stale daily logs — and it leads
  with the finding that matters most, in dollars.
- **Ask across every job at once.** "Which jobs have unbilled change orders?"
  "What's behind schedule this week?" One sentence instead of thirty minutes of
  clicking through jobs.
- **It shows its work.** You watch it read your actual budget and schedule
  before it answers — and it will not state a dollar figure it didn't just read
  from your data. When it explains how to do something in JobTread, it cites
  the exact moment in JobTread's own tutorial videos.
- **Safe by design.** The Assistant reads; humans decide. Any change it
  proposes arrives as a draft for your approval — it never edits your data on
  its own. It works only for your company, only in JobTread.

Like every JT Power Tools tier, one subscription covers your **whole
company** — every teammate with the extension gets the Assistant. No per-seat
math, no policing logins.

### 🚀 Assistant Pro — $249/mo, covers your whole company

Everything in Assistant, with more horsepower and true automation:

- **Deep analysis mode** on our most powerful AI model for the heavy jobs:

- **A larger usage pool** sized for teams that lean on it all day.

*Fair-usage note (both Assistant tiers): each company gets a generous monthly

## How it works

1. Subscribe and you'll get a license key instantly.
2. Install the extension (Chrome Web Store link) and paste in your key.
3. Create your free account at app.jtpowertools.com to connect your JobTread
   org (takes ~2 minutes; this is what powers the API and AI features).
4. Upgrading later? Just switch your plan — same license key, features light up
   immediately.

## Common questions

**Does my data leave JobTread?** No. The extension works inside JobTread's own
pages. API and AI features read your data through a JobTread grant key *you*
create and can revoke anytime — we never see your JobTread password.

**Can the AI change my data?** Not without a human. Every proposed change is a
draft until someone on your team approves it.

**Which browsers?** Chrome and Edge fully supported; Firefox supported.

**Per seat or per company?** Per company — every tier. One subscription
covers your whole team; invite them through the portal and everyone's
features light up. No per-seat pricing anywhere.

**Can I cancel anytime?** Yes. Your tier keeps working until the end of the
billing period. The Free features are yours forever regardless.
