Inspect for AI

Highlights a JobTread element so you can pick the exact thing to send to your AI.

Build with AI — copies a prompt to paste into your own AI chat; load the result with “Paste from AI” below.
Shortcuts on JobTread: Alt+click an element, Alt+Shift+P for picker, Alt+Shift+M for multi-view.

How tweaks work

  1. Turn on Inspect for AI above.
  2. Click Pick an element on JobTread, then click the thing you want to change.
  3. The DOM context is on your clipboard — paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor and describe what you want.
  4. Hit Paste from AI and paste the JSON it gives you back.
